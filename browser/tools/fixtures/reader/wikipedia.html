Marta Kell

Fictional clockmaker
For the river, see Kell (river).
Born1801, Lowmoor

Marta Kell (/ˈmɑːrtə kɛl/; listen; 1801–1874; UK: /kɛl/, US: /kɛːl/) was a clockmaker from Lowmoor.[1] She built the tower clock of the old mill.[citation needed]

Early life

[edit]

Kell grew up above her father's workshop, where she learned to file gears by hand.[2]
