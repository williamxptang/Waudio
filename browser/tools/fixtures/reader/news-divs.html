Harbour pilots stop work over night shifts

By Ann Reed

Get the week's shipping news in your inbox with the Harbour Brief newsletter. Sign up here.

PORTLOW, Nov 12 (Newswire) - Harbour pilots in Portlow stopped work on Wednesday after the port changed their night shifts.
The pilots guide every large ship through the narrow channel, so no ship can enter or leave.
The port said talks would start on Friday and that it hoped to reopen the channel by the weekend.

Reporting by Ann Reed in Portlow; editing by Tom Hale

Ann Reed covers shipping and ports for Newswire from Portlow, where she has lived for ten years.
